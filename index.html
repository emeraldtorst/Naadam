Can I work while studying in Austria?

Yes! Students with a valid residence permit can work up to 20 hours per week during the semester. You need a work permit (Beschäftigungsbewilligung), which your employer usually applies for at AMS.

Where can I find affordable student housing in Austria?

Apply early — housing is usually first-come, first-served. Popular student dorm providers: ÖJAB – affordable, well-connected locations. STUWO – includes utilities, WiFi, and often fitness rooms. Home4Students – shared or single rooms, from €299–€469/month. Akademikerhilfe – wide network of dorms, prices vary (€388–€630/month)

Do I need to register my address after arriving in Austria?

Yes. All residents in Austria — including students — must register their address. Go to the local Meldeamt within 72 hours of arrival. You’ll need your passport, rental contract, and a completed Meldezettel form. The Meldezettel is required for many other processes, like getting a residence permit, opening a bank account, or applying for health insurance.

How do I find private accommodation if dorms are full?

Popular platforms: Willhaben – Austria’s largest housing site. WG-Gesucht – for shared apartments. Facebook groups such as “Wohnungen in Wien”. very package includes a set number of revisions to ensure you’re happy with the final result. Additional revisions can be added if needed.

How do I open a bank account in Austria as a student?

Popular banks for students: Erste Bank, Bank Austria, BAWAG, Volksbank. Usually you need: Passport Meldezettel (address registration) Proof of student status (Studienbestätigung) Student accounts are often free of monthly fees. Having an Austrian bank account is required for residence permit applications and health insurance payments.

I want to join the AMOX Association. How can I become a member?

We’re always happy to welcome new members! 🎉 Please apply through the contact form on our official website. Once we receive your application, our team will get in touch with you with the next steps.
